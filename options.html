Escout — Etsy API key

Tags (part of COPY META DATA) and the optional Shop Info overlay need your own free Etsy Open API v3 credentials. Everything else in Escout works without this.

  1. Go to etsy.com/developers/register and sign in with your Etsy account.
  2. Create a new app — pick Personal Access, not commercial. No business details needed.
  3. Copy the Keystring shown for your new app.
  4. Click Show shared secret next to it and copy that too.
  5. Paste both below and click Save.

Stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.sync) — never sent anywhere except directly to Etsy's own API. A personal Etsy app is capped at 5 requests/second and 5,000/day, which comfortably covers normal use of this extension.
